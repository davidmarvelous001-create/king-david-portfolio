Case study · Branding & packaging

Delight
Hibiscus

A hibiscus drink with a name to live up to. The label had to look as real as the ingredients before anyone read a word — and it had to be print ready in 24 hours.

Client
Asaolu Olayinka
Delight Natural Drinks
Role
Design lead — identity, packaging, art direction
Year
2026
Scope
Identity · Packaging · Launch
24 hrs From brief to print ready artwork
1 label Scaled across the bottle range without a redraw
2 claims 100% natural and zero sugar, proved by design rather than a paragraph
01 The problem

A new drink gets one glance on a shelf.

Delight was launching a hibiscus drink into a market where every bottle promises refreshment. The label had seconds to answer three questions: is it natural, is it really hibiscus, and is it just another sugary soft drink?

The name was already doing the emotional work. The packaging had to make it believable.

02 The constraint

One label. Print ready in a day.

There was no bespoke bottle and no time for a second round. Every decision had to survive production: print safe colour, one label system that could scale across the range, and artwork that stayed legible at arm's length in a fridge.

  1. 01A flat label that wraps without losing the name.
  2. 02Ingredients drawn from the actual contents — hibiscus, dates, ginger, pineapple, watermelon, cinnamon.
  3. 03A red, green and white palette that reads natural before it reads sweet.

If the ingredients are the proof, put them on the label.

Working note, first review
03 Previous look

The sticker it replaced.

The old sticker carried everything at once: the logo, the name, the full ingredient list, two contact routes, a zero sugar badge, a storage note and a shake instruction. Every element was true. They all spoke at the same volume.

The rebuild kept what already worked — the script, the red and green, the stamp language — then gave it an order to follow.

04 The idea

Let the product name do the talking.

The wordmark stays quiet at the top. Hibiscus Drink takes the centre in hand drawn lettering, with "Refreshing" and "Keep Refrigerated" arcing around it like a stamp. The claims sit below in plain language: 100% natural, zero sugar, and the full ingredient list.

The bottle is the hero. The label frames it instead of competing with it — and every mark answers a question a first time buyer would ask.

The name promises delight. The label proves it.

Packaging principle Delight Natural Drinks · 2026
05 The work

One system, every surface.

The same artwork carried from the studio bottle shot to the flat print file and the lifestyle campaign, so the drink looked like itself everywhere it showed up.

06 The result

Print ready in a day. Consistent from the first bottle.

The label went from brief to print ready artwork in 24 hours. The same file scaled across the bottle range without a redraw, and held together from studio shot to fridge shelf.

The design doesn't ask anyone to trust a claim. It shows the ingredients, states the proof, and lets the drink look like what it is.
