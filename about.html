About

Who is
King David?

Graphic designer, team lead, and the one who notices when the logo is 2px off.

I'm David Olufemi, a graphic designer with six years of practice and 100+ projects behind me.

I lead the design team at Velox Real Estate, building campaigns for Cadenza, Bora, Gardenia and Luxe: billboards, brochures, social and packaging.

Before that, years of freelance work for drinks, food and Web3 brands. I care about clarity first: good design should be understood before it's admired.
