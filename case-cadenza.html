Case study · Real estate campaign

Cadenza
by Velox

₦20M plots off Obiri-Ikwere, sold to families who had never bought land before. The board had one job: make the promise believable before the traffic moved.

Client
Velox Real Estate and Investment Limited
Role
Design lead — concept, art direction, copy
Year
2026
Scope
Out-of-home · Print · Social
8% More property enquiries in the three weeks after launch
₦20M Entry price, printed at billboard scale instead of buried in a brochure
4.2 days From brief to first round of concepts
01 The problem

A plot is a big promise. The board gets three seconds.

Cadenza was selling ₦20M plots to first-time buyers. The objection was never the location — it was trust. Is this real? Is the title clean? Can I actually afford it?

Most land campaigns save those answers for the brochure, after the click. This one had to answer them before the traffic moved, in one glance, from the passenger seat.

02 The constraint

One board. No second chance to explain.

Every element had a fixed reading order: stop the eye, state the price, remove the fear. If a line didn't do one of those three jobs, it came off.

The boards ran in Abuja and Port Harcourt while the estate itself sits off Obiri-Ikwere. That distance is why the address block carries a phone number and a web address instead of a postcode — a buyer had to be able to act from the passenger seat.

  1. 01A professional whose smile and thumbs up signalled confidence in the deal — not a staged family.
  2. 02The price at billboard scale — no "from" in 8pt type.
  3. 03The C of O stated as a fact, not a footnote.

If the price is the objection, the price should be the headline.

Working note, first review
03 The idea

Sell the outcome. Prove it in the same breath.

The line does the emotional work: Your Family Deserves a Plot. The image does the confidence work: a smiling professional, thumbs up, the deal reading as sound before anyone says a word. Then the price and the title sit locked to the bottom edge, where the eye lands last and the last objection gets answered.

The family lives in the line. The confidence lives in the face. Nothing decorative survived — the board reads in one pass at speed, and again on foot.

Your Family Deserves a Plot.

Campaign line Velox Real Estate and Investment Limited · 2026
04 The work

One line, every surface.

The same hierarchy carried from the board master down to the street-level install and the social crop, so the campaign never had to be re-explained.

05 The result

The board paid for itself in enquiries.

Three weeks after launch, Cadenza saw 8% more property enquiries than the same period before. The answer to the first phone call was already printed on the board — C of O in View, not buried on page four of a brochure.

That is the whole argument for design that starts with the objection instead of the mood board.
